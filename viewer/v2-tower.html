    Where the numbers come from. Every default is from v2-two-tier-spec.md: column Ø28, tier 1 at z=84, trough 16 mm deep with 3 mm walls, 10 mm of water in the trough, tier 2 at 0.65× the tier 1 diameter, 4 plants below and 2 above. The mesh base is the V1 part, reused unchanged.

    Why the airlift rise is a hard ceiling. An air-stone lift raises water by roughly 10–20 % of how deep the tube is submerged — not by how powerful the pump is. The glass only holds ~74 mm of water, so a 1 W pump and a 5 W pump reach the same height; the bigger one just bubbles harder. Measure it before anything is designed: a clear Ø6–8 mm tube with the air stone at its foot, standing in the filled glass, and read how far above the water the flow comes out.

    The glass profile is interpolated, not measured. Inner mouth Ø90 and 80 mm from the base to the low side of the slanted rim are real measurements; the bulge of the body and the curve of the base are guesses from a photograph.
